## 🧑‍💻 About Me

I'm a **cybersecurity analyst** with hands-on experience in **security monitoring, threat detection, and incident response** across financial services, healthcare, and industry research. I've hunted threats in **Splunk**, built phishing defenses for a credit union, supported GRC and access reviews at a major health insurer, and today I research **SIEM modernization, AI-driven security operations, and identity security** for CISOs.

I care about turning complex security topics into clear, actionable guidance, whether that's a SOC detection, a risk assessment, or an article read by thousands of banking professionals.

- 💬 **Ask me about:** threat intelligence, MITRE ATT&CK, phishing defense, Splunk
